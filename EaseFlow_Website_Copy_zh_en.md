# EaseFlow 隐私政策

**修订日期：** 2026 年 10 月 31 日

**适用版本：** 当前上架版本为 v1.0；以下 v1.1 功能及数据处理说明自 v1.1 正式发布时适用。

**开发者：** Pan XinYue

**联系邮箱：** easeflowsupport@163.com

### 1. 概述

EaseFlow 尊重并重视你的隐私。

我们希望以简单、透明的方式提供拉伸和运动指导服务。本政策说明 EaseFlow 如何处理应用内的信息，以及你访问官方网站或联系技术支持时可能涉及的数据。

### 2. 应用内的数据处理

EaseFlow v1.1 无需注册账号。应用在设备本地保存和处理：

- 收藏的动作和方案标识；
- 已完成训练的记录，包括完成时间、动作或方案标识、训练模式、标题快照和实际活动时长；
- 应用语言、外观模式、默认语音指导，以及每日拉伸与定时活动提醒的设置。

累计训练次数、累计活动时长、近 7 天或 30 天的趋势及训练日历由设备上的训练记录计算。搜索、筛选、计时与进行中的训练状态也在设备本地处理。应用不提供账号云同步，也不建立由开发者维护的云端训练档案。

提醒默认关闭。开启每日拉伸或定时活动提醒时，应用请求 iOS 通知权限，并按选定时间向 iOS 提交重复的本地通知。定时活动提醒按选定间隔和时段安排，不会监测你是否久坐。通知由 iOS 调度和送达，可能受系统设置影响。

小、中、大三种桌面小组件使用随应用提供的方案资料；你可在小组件配置中选择偏好方案。小组件显示方案名称、时长和动作数量，并可打开相应方案或方案列表；它不显示训练记录，也不读取提醒设置。小组件的选择由 iOS 管理。

训练记录保存在应用的本地文件中，收藏和偏好保存在设备上的应用设置中。这些本地数据可能包含在设备或系统备份中；是否以及如何备份、传输和恢复取决于你的 iOS 与备份服务设置。应用未设置训练记录文件的备份排除标记，因此不能保证这些数据绝对不会离开设备。

### 3. 数据收集与共享

EaseFlow v1.1：

- 不集成广告、用户追踪或第三方分析 SDK。
- 不在训练、收藏、记录、Widget 或提醒功能中向开发者服务器上传这些本地数据。
- 不访问 HealthKit 健康记录、定位、麦克风、相机或通讯录。
- 不出售用户个人信息。

未来如果应用增加需要收集数据的功能，我们会更新本政策，并按照适用要求提供必要说明和选择。

如果你主动打开应用中的网站、隐私政策或客服链接，系统会使用浏览器或邮件服务访问相应服务；相关网络与邮件信息的处理见下文。

### 4. 语音指导

EaseFlow 使用 Apple 提供的系统语音合成功能朗读训练指引。

应用不会为此录制声音、识别你的语音或访问麦克风。语音合成由设备系统处理，系统语音资源的获取和管理可能由 Apple 根据设备设置完成。

### 5. 官方网站

EaseFlow 官方网站由 GitHub Pages 提供静态页面。

网站不主动接入广告追踪或第三方用户分析工具。

作为网站托管服务，GitHub 可能为安全及服务运行目的处理必要的技术信息，包括访问者 IP 地址。相关处理受 GitHub 的隐私政策约束。

### 6. 联系技术支持

如果你主动通过电子邮件联系我们，我们可能收到你的：

- 电子邮箱地址
- 邮件内容
- 你主动提供的截图或附件

这些信息仅用于处理咨询、问题反馈及相关支持请求，不用于广告营销。

支持邮件通过电子邮件服务提供商传输和保存。客服邮箱由开发者本人管理，可查找及删除相关邮件；邮件服务商对备份副本的处理遵循其自身规则。

你可以通过客服邮箱提出合理的个人信息删除请求。开发者会核查并处理可删除的相关邮件；对于依法需要保留的信息，按适用规定处理。从邮箱删除邮件不保证服务商备份立即彻底清除。

请尽量不要在反馈邮件中发送不必要的敏感个人信息。

### 7. 信息删除与管理

你可以在「我的」中取消动作或方案收藏，并修改语言、外观、默认语音指导和提醒偏好。你也可以在 iOS 系统设置中管理或关闭 EaseFlow 的通知权限。

在「记录」中，你可以打开完整训练历史，逐条确认删除训练记录。成功删除后，应用会根据剩余记录重新计算累计数据、趋势和日历。当前没有应用内一键删除全部记录的入口。

如果训练记录的主文件保存失败，应用会尝试把待保存记录写入设备上的后备恢复文件，并在之后重试；恢复文件不可写或数据损坏时，记录仍可能丢失。应用不会覆盖无法读取的原文件，也不保证自动恢复成功。删除记录时，应用也会处理后备文件中的对应记录；若写入或删除失败，界面可能提示存储问题，删除未必完成。

卸载应用通常会从当前设备中删除应用的本地数据，但由操作系统或备份服务管理的副本可能仍然存在，具体取决于设备和备份设置。

EaseFlow 不维护可供远程删除的云端训练档案。

对于你主动提供的客服邮件信息，可以联系我们提出访问或删除请求。

随着应用功能和相关服务的发展，我们可能更新本隐私政策。

更新后的版本将在官方网站公布，并标明修订日期和适用版本。

# EaseFlow Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 31, 2026

**Applies To:** v1.0 is currently available on the App Store. The v1.1 features and data practices described below apply when v1.1 is released.

**Developer:** Pan XinYue

**Contact:** easeflowsupport@163.com

### 1. Overview

EaseFlow respects your privacy.

We aim to provide a simple and transparent stretching experience. This policy explains how information is handled when you use EaseFlow, visit our website, or contact support.

### 2. Information Stored on Your Device

EaseFlow v1.1 does not require an account. The app stores and processes the following on your device:

- Identifiers of exercises and routines you have saved as favorites;
- Completed session records, including completion time, exercise or routine identifier, session mode, a snapshot of its title, and actual active duration;
- App language, appearance mode, default voice guidance, and daily stretch and scheduled activity reminder settings.

Total session count, total active time, the 7-day or 30-day trends, and the training calendar are calculated from records on your device. Searches, filters, timers, and in-progress session state are also handled locally. The app does not offer account-based cloud sync or maintain a developer-hosted workout profile.

Reminders are off by default. When you enable daily stretch or scheduled activity reminders, the app requests iOS notification permission and submits recurring local notifications at your chosen times. Activity reminders follow your selected interval and time window; they do not detect whether you have been sitting. iOS schedules and delivers the notifications, which may be affected by system settings.

The small, medium, and large Home Screen widgets use routine information bundled with the app. You can choose a preferred routine in the widget configuration. Widgets show routine titles, durations, and exercise counts, and can open a routine or the routine list. They do not display workout history or read reminder settings. iOS manages the widget selection.

Session records are stored in local app files. Favorites and preferences are stored in app settings on the device. This local data may be included in device or system backups; whether and how it is backed up, transferred, or restored depends on your iOS and backup service settings. The app does not mark the session record files as excluded from backup, so we cannot guarantee that the data will never leave your device.

### 3. Data Collection and Sharing

EaseFlow v1.1:

- Does not include advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.
- Does not upload this local data to developer-operated servers through its training, favorites, records, widget, or reminder features.
- Does not access HealthKit records, location, microphone, camera, or contacts.
- Does not sell personal information.

If future features require additional data processing, we will update this policy and provide any notices or choices required by applicable law.

If you choose to open a website, privacy policy, or support link in the app, the system uses a browser or email service to reach it. The relevant network and email processing is described below.

### 4. Voice Guidance

EaseFlow uses Apple's system speech synthesis to read exercise instructions aloud.

The app does not record your voice, recognize speech, or access your microphone for this feature. Speech synthesis is handled by the device operating system. Apple may manage or download system voice resources according to your device settings.

### 5. Our Website

The EaseFlow website is hosted on GitHub Pages.

We do not intentionally integrate advertising trackers or third-party analytics into the website.

GitHub, as the hosting provider, may process technical information, including visitor IP addresses, for security and service operations. Such processing is subject to GitHub's privacy policies.

### 6. Contacting Support

If you contact us by email, we may receive:

- Your email address
- The contents of your message
- Screenshots or attachments you choose to provide

We use this information only to respond to inquiries, investigate issues, and provide support. We do not use support correspondence for advertising.

Support emails are transmitted and stored through an email service provider. The developer personally manages the support mailbox and can locate and delete relevant messages. The email provider's own policies govern how it handles backup copies.

You may send a reasonable request to delete personal information in support emails. The developer will review the request and delete messages that can be deleted, subject to applicable legal retention requirements. Deleting a message from the mailbox does not guarantee immediate, complete removal from the provider's backups.

Please avoid including unnecessary sensitive personal information in support messages.

### 7. Managing and Deleting Information

In Me, you can remove an exercise or routine from favorites and change your app language, appearance, default voice guidance, and reminder preferences. You can also manage or disable EaseFlow's notification permission in iOS Settings.

In Records, you can open the full session history and confirm deletion of individual records. After a successful deletion, the app recalculates totals, trends, and the calendar from the remaining records. There is currently no in-app action to delete all records at once.

If saving the main session history file fails, the app attempts to write pending records to a local recovery file and retries later. Records may still be lost if that file cannot be written or the data is damaged. The app does not overwrite unreadable files and cannot guarantee automatic recovery. When deleting a record, the app also handles a matching entry in the recovery file. A storage error may prevent deletion and be shown in the app.

Uninstalling the app normally removes its local data from the device. Copies may remain in operating system backups, depending on your backup settings.

EaseFlow does not maintain a server-side workout profile that can be remotely deleted.

You may contact us to request access to or deletion of information voluntarily provided through support emails.

### 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as EaseFlow and its related services evolve.

The latest version will be published on our website with its revision date and applicable app version.

### 常见问题

#### 五个 Tab 分别有什么功能？

「首页」提供快捷入口和推荐；「动作」用于浏览、搜索和筛选动作；「方案」用于查看并开始拉伸方案；「记录」显示累计数据、趋势、日历和完整训练历史；「我的」可查看收藏并调整偏好，也可进入「关于 EaseFlow」查找网站、帮助、隐私政策和使用条款。

#### 如何切换语言、外观和语音指导？

打开「我的」：在「应用语言」中选择跟随系统或指定语言；在「外观模式」中选择浅色、深色或跟随系统；使用「默认语音指导」开关调整新训练的语音默认值。部分训练也可单独选择有声或静音。

#### 如何设置每日拉伸和定时活动提醒？

打开「我的」→「提醒设置」。每日拉伸提醒可设置开关和时间；定时活动提醒可设置开关、60／90／120 分钟间隔及开始、结束时间。首次开启时请允许 iOS 通知。活动提醒按计划发送，不检测你是否真的久坐。

#### 已开启提醒却没有收到通知怎么办？

先检查「我的」→「提醒设置」中的开关、时间和活动时段是否有效，再检查 iOS 系统设置中的 EaseFlow 通知权限、声音和专注模式。若曾拒绝权限，可从应用内提示打开 iOS 设置；系统可能延迟或抑制通知。

#### 如何收藏或取消收藏动作和方案？

在动作或方案详情页使用收藏按钮，再到「我的」→「我的收藏」查看。再次使用详情页中的收藏按钮即可取消收藏。收藏保存在本机；取消收藏不会删除既有训练记录。

#### 如何查看或删除训练历史？

完成的训练会出现在「记录」→「完整训练历史」。记录显示完成时间、模式和实际活动时长。使用单条记录的删除按钮并确认后，记录会从本机历史中移除；目前没有一键清空全部记录的入口。如出现存储错误，请留意「记录」页提示，删除可能尚未完成。

#### 趋势图、训练日历和「再次训练」如何使用？

「记录」页可切换近 7 天或 30 天的训练次数与活动时长趋势，并按月份查看日历；选择日期可查看当天记录。完整训练历史中的「再次训练」会打开仍可用的动作或方案详情，供你重新开始。日期按设备当前时区显示，跨午夜训练计入完成日。

#### 训练记录提示保存失败怎么办？

在「记录」页查看存储提示；如果出现「重试保存」，可点击重试。应用会尝试保留待保存记录并在之后恢复，但设备存储故障或恢复文件损坏时仍可能丢失记录。

#### 如何添加和配置桌面小组件？

在 iOS 主屏幕添加 EaseFlow 小组件，可选择小、中、大尺寸；通过 iOS 的小组件编辑入口选择偏好方案。小组件使用随应用提供的方案资料，点按可打开方案或方案列表，不显示你的训练记录。

#### 为什么有些动作需要手动继续？

部分训练需要手动确认，让你有时间调整姿势，并按照自己的节奏完成动作。

#### 语音指导没有声音怎么办？

请先检查设备音量、应用中的语音设置，以及设备是否提供当前语言的系统语音。

即使无法播放语音，你仍然可以通过屏幕指导和手动操作继续训练。

#### 使用时感到疼痛怎么办？

请立即停止引起明显疼痛或异常不适的动作。

EaseFlow 不能替代医疗建议。如有持续疼痛、麻木或其他健康问题，请咨询医疗专业人士。

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### What can I do in each of the five tabs?

Home offers shortcuts and recommendations. Exercises lets you browse, search, and filter movements. Routines lets you explore and start stretching routines. Records shows totals, trends, the calendar, and full training history. Me contains favorites and preferences; its About EaseFlow page links to the website, help, privacy policy, and terms.

#### How do I change the language, appearance, and voice guidance?

Open Me. Under App language, choose a language or follow your system setting. Under Appearance, choose Light, Dark, or Follow System. Use Default voice guidance to set the default for new sessions. Some sessions also let you choose voice or silence separately.

#### How do I set daily stretch and scheduled activity reminders?

Open Me → Reminders. You can set a time for daily stretches, or choose a 60, 90, or 120 minute interval and a start and end time for activity reminders. Allow iOS notifications when prompted. Activity reminders follow a schedule; they do not detect whether you have been sitting.

#### Why am I not receiving reminders?

Check the switches, times, and valid activity window in Me → Reminders. Then check EaseFlow's notification permission, sound settings, and Focus settings in iOS. If you previously denied permission, use the in-app prompt to open iOS Settings. iOS may delay or suppress notifications.

#### How do I add or remove favorites?

Use the favorite button on an exercise or routine detail page, then view saved items under Me → Favorites. Use the button again to remove a favorite. Favorites are stored on your device; removing one does not delete past training records.

#### How do I view or delete session history?

Completed sessions appear under Records → Full training history. Records show the completion time, mode, and actual active duration. Use a record's delete button and confirm to remove it from local history. There is currently no Delete All action. If you see a storage error, check the notice in Records; the deletion may not have completed.

#### How do trends, the training calendar, and Train Again work?

In Records, switch between 7-day and 30-day trends for session count or active time. Browse the calendar by month and select a date to see its sessions. Train Again in the full history opens an exercise or routine detail page when that content is still available. Dates use your device's current time zone; sessions crossing midnight count on their completion date.

#### What if a session record fails to save?

Check the storage notice in Records and tap Retry Save if offered. The app attempts to keep pending records for later recovery, but storage failures or damaged recovery data can still result in lost records.

#### How do I add and configure a Home Screen widget?

Add an EaseFlow widget from the iOS Home Screen in a small, medium, or large size. Use iOS's widget editing controls to choose a preferred routine. The widget uses routine information bundled with the app and can open a routine or the routine list. It does not display your session history.

#### Why do some exercises require manual confirmation?

Some sessions require confirmation so you have time to adjust your position and move at your own pace.

#### What if voice guidance isn't working?

Check your device volume, the app's voice guidance settings, and whether a system voice is available for your selected language.

You can still follow the on-screen instructions and continue manually without audio.

#### What should I do if an exercise hurts?

Stop any movement that causes significant pain or unusual discomfort.

EaseFlow is not a substitute for medical advice. If pain, numbness, or other concerning symptoms persist, consult a healthcare professional.
