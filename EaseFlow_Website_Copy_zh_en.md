# EaseFlow 隐私政策

**修订日期：** 2026 年 10 月 25 日

**适用版本：** EaseFlow v1.1（自该版本发布时起）

**开发者：** Pan XinYue

**联系邮箱：** easeflowsupport@163.com

### 1. 概述

EaseFlow 尊重并重视你的隐私。

我们希望以简单、透明的方式提供拉伸和运动指导服务。本政策说明 EaseFlow 如何处理应用内的信息，以及你访问官方网站或联系技术支持时可能涉及的数据。

### 2. 应用内的数据处理

EaseFlow v1.1 无需注册账号。

应用仅在你的设备上保存以下偏好设置：

- 选择的应用语言
- 默认语音指导开关
- 每日拉伸提醒的开关和提醒时间
- 定时活动提醒的开关、间隔及开始和结束时间

这些信息用于保存你的使用偏好。提醒偏好保存在设备的应用设置中，不会由应用上传至开发者可访问的服务器。

提醒功能默认关闭。开启每日拉伸提醒或定时活动提醒时，应用会请求 iOS 通知权限；获得授权后，应用根据你设置的时间在设备上向 iOS 提交重复的本地通知。定时活动提醒按设定的间隔和时段安排，不会实时监测你是否久坐。通知由 iOS 系统调度和送达，实际显示时间受系统通知设置影响。

桌面小组件展示随应用提供的一套现有拉伸方案的名称、时长和动作数量。点击小组件会通过设备上的应用链接打开相应方案；小组件不需要读取提醒偏好。

搜索、筛选、动作选择、计时与训练进度均在设备本地处理。当前版本不保存长期训练历史或个人健康档案，也不提供账号云同步功能。

### 3. 数据收集与共享

EaseFlow 当前版本：

- 不集成广告、用户追踪或第三方分析 SDK。
- 不通过应用向开发者服务器上传用户个人信息。
- 不访问 HealthKit 健康记录、定位、麦克风、相机或通讯录。
- 不出售用户个人信息。

未来如果应用增加需要收集数据的功能，我们会更新本政策，并按照适用要求提供必要说明和选择。

### 4. 语音指导

EaseFlow 使用 Apple 提供的系统语音合成功能朗读训练指引。

应用不会为此录制声音、识别你的语音或访问麦克风。语音合成由设备系统处理，系统语音资源的获取和管理可能由 Apple 根据设备设置完成。

### 5. 官方网站

EaseFlow 官方网站计划使用 GitHub Pages 提供静态页面。

网站不主动接入广告追踪或第三方用户分析工具。

作为网站托管服务，GitHub 可能为安全及服务运行目的处理必要的技术信息，包括访问者 IP 地址。相关处理受 GitHub 的隐私政策约束。

### 6. 联系技术支持

如果你主动通过电子邮件联系我们，我们可能收到你的：

- 电子邮箱地址
- 邮件内容
- 你主动提供的截图或附件

这些信息仅用于处理咨询、问题反馈及相关支持请求，不用于广告营销。

支持邮件通过电子邮件服务提供商传输和保存。我们仅在处理请求及履行适用法律义务所需的期间保留相关通信。

你可以通过客服邮箱申请删除支持邮件中的个人信息。对于法律要求保留的信息，我们将按适用规定处理。

请尽量不要在反馈邮件中发送不必要的敏感个人信息。

### 7. 信息删除与管理

你可以随时在应用设置中修改语言、默认语音指导和提醒偏好，包括关闭提醒或调整时间、间隔和时段。你也可以在 iOS 系统设置中管理或关闭 EaseFlow 的通知权限。

卸载应用通常会从当前设备中删除应用的本地数据，但由操作系统或备份服务管理的副本可能仍然存在，具体取决于设备和备份设置。

EaseFlow 不维护可供远程删除的云端训练档案。

对于你主动提供的客服邮件信息，可以联系我们提出访问或删除请求。

随着应用功能和相关服务的发展，我们可能更新本隐私政策。

更新后的版本将在官方网站公布，并标明修订日期和适用版本。

**Last Updated:** October 25, 2026

### 1. Overview

EaseFlow respects your privacy.

We aim to provide a simple and transparent stretching experience. This policy explains how information is handled when you use EaseFlow, visit our website, or contact support.

### 2. Information Stored on Your Device

EaseFlow v1.1 does not require an account.

The app stores the following preferences on your device:

- Your selected app language
- Your default voice guidance setting
- Whether daily stretch reminders are enabled and the time you choose
- Whether scheduled activity reminders are enabled, their interval, and their start and end times

These settings save your preferences. Reminder preferences are stored in the app's settings on your device and are not uploaded by the app to servers accessible to the developer.

Reminders are off by default. When you turn on daily stretch or scheduled activity reminders, the app requests iOS notification permission. If you allow notifications, the app submits recurring local notifications to iOS based on your chosen times. Activity reminders follow your selected interval and time window; they do not monitor whether you have been sitting. iOS schedules and delivers the notifications, and system notification settings may affect when they appear.

The Home Screen widget displays the title, duration, and exercise count of an existing stretching routine bundled with the app. Tapping it opens that routine through a link handled on your device. The widget does not need access to your reminder preferences.

Searches, filters, exercise selections, timers, and session progress are processed locally. The current version does not maintain a permanent workout history or personal health profile, and does not offer account-based cloud syncing.

### 3. Data Collection and Sharing

The current version of EaseFlow:

- Does not include advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.
- Does not upload personal information to developer-operated servers.
- Does not access HealthKit records, location, microphone, camera, or contacts.
- Does not sell personal information.

If future features require additional data processing, we will update this policy and provide any notices or choices required by applicable law.

### 4. Voice Guidance

EaseFlow uses Apple's system speech synthesis to read exercise instructions aloud.

The app does not record your voice, recognize speech, or access your microphone for this feature. Speech synthesis is handled by the device operating system. Apple may manage or download system voice resources according to your device settings.

The EaseFlow website is planned to be hosted on GitHub Pages.

We do not intentionally integrate advertising trackers or third-party analytics into the website.

You may request deletion of personal information contained in support correspondence, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Please avoid including unnecessary sensitive personal information in support messages.

### 7. Managing and Deleting Information

You can change your app language, default voice guidance, and reminder preferences in EaseFlow settings, including turning reminders off or changing their times, interval, and time window. You can also manage or disable EaseFlow's notification permission in iOS Settings.

Uninstalling the app normally removes its local data from the device. Copies may remain in operating system backups, depending on your backup settings.

EaseFlow does not maintain a server-side workout profile that can be remotely deleted.

You may contact us to request access to or deletion of information voluntarily provided through support emails.

### 常见问题

#### 如何切换应用语言？

打开 EaseFlow → 设置 → 语言。

你可以选择跟随系统，或独立选择自己喜欢的应用语言，无需修改设备的系统语言。

#### 如何关闭语音指导？

打开设置，在「训练偏好」中关闭默认语音指导。

这会影响之后新开始的训练。部分训练也可以单独选择有声或静音。

#### 为什么有些动作需要手动继续？

EaseFlow 的部分训练采用手动确认方式，让你有足够时间调整姿势，并按照自己的节奏完成动作。

#### EaseFlow 会保存我的训练记录吗？

当前版本不提供长期训练历史功能，也无需注册账号。

#### 语音指导没有声音怎么办？

请先检查设备音量、应用中的语音设置，以及设备是否提供当前语言的系统语音。

即使无法播放语音，你仍然可以通过屏幕指导和手动操作继续训练。

#### 使用时感到疼痛怎么办？

请立即停止引起明显疼痛或异常不适的动作。

EaseFlow 不能替代医疗建议。如有持续疼痛、麻木或其他健康问题，请咨询医疗专业人士。

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### How do I change the app language?

Open EaseFlow → Settings → Language.

You can follow your system language or select a language for EaseFlow independently.

#### How do I turn off voice guidance?

Open Settings and turn off Default Voice Guidance under Training Preferences.

This changes the default for new sessions. Some sessions also allow you to choose voice guidance individually.

#### Why do some exercises require manual confirmation?

Some guided exercises use manual confirmation so you have enough time to adjust your position and move at your own pace.

#### Does EaseFlow save my workout history?

The current version does not store a permanent workout history or require an account.

#### What if voice guidance isn't working?

Check your device volume, the app's voice guidance settings, and whether a system voice is available for your selected language.

You can still follow the on-screen instructions and continue manually without audio.

#### What should I do if an exercise hurts?

Stop any movement that causes significant pain or unusual discomfort.

EaseFlow is not a substitute for medical advice. If pain, numbness, or other concerning symptoms persist, consult a healthcare professional.
